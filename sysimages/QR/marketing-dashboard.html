💡 Marketing Best Practices

✅ What Works

  • Clear CTA: "SCAN TO REGISTER" is stronger than "Scan QR"
  • Action verbs: SCAN, JOIN, REGISTER, DISCOVER, TRANSFORM
  • Emotional hooks: "Transform," "Fellowship," "Impact"
  • Biblical messaging: Adds credibility and appeals to audience
  • Urgency words: "Limited," "Now," "Today," "Filling fast"
  • Mobile-first: 90%+ of traffic will be mobile
  • Consistent branding: Use pink #E91E63 across all QR codes

❌ What Doesn't Work

  • ❌ Generic "Scan this QR" without context
  • ❌ QR codes that point to unclear destinations
  • ❌ Posting same message on all platforms (customize per platform)
  • ❌ Inconsistent timing (post multiple times same day = poor results)
  • ❌ No tracking = no way to improve
  • ❌ Ignoring platform differences (Instagram ≠ LinkedIn tone)
