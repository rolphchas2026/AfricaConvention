Internal Committee Document  ·  Africa Convention 2026

This briefing was prepared ahead of the general committee meeting to evaluate two proposed early-bird discount structures for the 2026 convention accommodation packages. The analysis below presents both options with full pricing matrices, a recommendation with supporting reasoning, and a review of the 10% hotel retention arrangement. These materials are intended as the main course of action for committee discussion and resolution.

✅ Committee Recommendation — OPTION B

1

Services carry real fixed costs the committee must absorb regardless of booking date. Lunch ($125/person) and commute ($100/person) are non-negotiable per-delegate operational costs. Discounting them creates a deficit the committee subsidises — costs that do not change whether a delegate books in May or June 16.

2

Badge printing ($10) and raffle donation ($5) are actual one-time expenditures per confirmed delegate. These are disbursed the moment a delegate is confirmed — discounting them would mean early delegates receive a fully-costed service at a loss to the committee, with no operational basis for the reduction.

3

Option B delivers a clean, defensible marketing proposition. "Book early and save on your hotel accommodation" is auditable, honest, and understood by delegates. Discounting lunch, commute and badge fees creates logical inconsistency — those costs are not hotel-dependent and any enquiring delegate will find the reasoning weak.

4

Stable services revenue protects the baseline operational budget. Under Option B, every confirmed delegate — regardless of booking tier — contributes a guaranteed $240 toward services. This is the committee's financial floor. Under Option A, that floor drops by up to $96/person at D-Tier, removing the budget reliability that operations and logistics planning depends on.

5

The 10% hotel retention margin is better protected under Option B. Retention income is derived from the accommodation component only. Under Option A, the committee is asked to simultaneously compress retention via the early-bird discount AND subsidise fixed service costs — a double squeeze on the revenue model. Option B limits compression to the accommodation margin where it originated.

🏨 10% Hotel Retention Fee — Anonymous Committee Arrangement

The committee has negotiated preferential group rates with the hosting hotel below their standard rack prices. The convention charges delegates the published convention rate. The spread between what delegates pay and what the hotel receives (10% of the convention rate per night) is retained by the committee as a coordination and administrative fee. This arrangement is confidential per the agreement with the hotel and is not disclosed to delegates.

🗒️ Quick Tutorial — How to Use This Dashboard

📊 Overview tab

See all registrations at a glance. Use the ✏️ button to edit an attendee, 🎫 to download their badge PDF, 📧 to email it, 📁 for their files, and 🗑️ to delete.

✅ Approval tab

Approve pending registrations here. Click Approve next to any attendee to mark them as APPROVED. Approved status is required before a badge can be emailed.

📥 Check-in tab

Type or scan a Ticket ID and press Enter to check in a delegate. The list below shows everyone already checked in with their arrival time.

📤 Check-out tab

Same as check-in — enter a Ticket ID and press Enter. Duration between check-in and check-out is calculated automatically.

📷 QR Scanner tab

Click ▶ Start Camera, point at a delegate's badge QR code — check-in fires instantly. Use Manual Entry below if camera is unavailable.

📁 Files per attendee

Click 📁 on any attendee row to open their file folder. Upload payment proof (PDF/image) or any supporting documents. Files are stored in the database.

📈 Statistics tab

Full attendee list with check-in status. Shows name, email, ticket type, accommodation package, organisation, check-in time and payment status. Same action buttons as Overview — use this for on-site headcount and bag-check during the convention.

🎰 Raffle tab

Controls the convention nomination game. Open and close voting, reveal winners, reset, and display the two venue QR codes (voting entry + results reveal). The Top 3 panel shows gold/silver/bronze leaders live. See the Raffle Game Operations section below for the full step-by-step playbook.

📅 Day-of Operations — Latest Features

New tools built for June 18–22, 2026 on-site coordination

🔵 FAB Quick-Scan Button

The pulsing circle at the bottom-right corner is your fastest check-in tool. Tap it from any tab — no navigation needed.

Steps:
1. Tap the pulsing 📷 SCAN button
2. Select Check In or Check Out using the toggle pill
3. Tap ▶ Start Camera
4. Point at the delegate's badge QR code
5. Result appears instantly — green = success, red = error
6. Tap ✕ or outside to close

🟢🟡 Status Colour Codes

Check-in rows are colour-coded in real time:

✅ Active — delegate is inside the venue

📤 Checked Out — delegate has left

The Check-in and Check-out lists update together whenever any scan fires.

📤 Check-out Tab Scanner

The Check-out tab now has its own camera — no need to use the QR Scanner tab.

Steps:
1. Open the Check-out tab
2. Tap ▶ Start Camera on the left panel
3. Scan badge → automatic checkout
4. Or type a Ticket ID manually + press Enter / tap Check Out

The Recent Check-outs table below updates immediately after every scan.

↩ Revoke Checkout

Accidentally checked someone out? Use Revoke to undo it — the delegate returns to Active / Checked In status immediately.

Where: Check-out tab → Recent Check-outs table → ↩ Revoke button on the row

Use case: Scanner mis-fired, delegate scanned from outside, or coordinator error. Revoke does not erase the check-in record — only clears the checkout.

🔧 Maintenance Mode

Takes the entire portal offline with a branded "Coming Back Soon" page — all routes show the maintenance screen, including the raffle game and home page. Static images still load so the page looks polished.

To activate:
1. Open Vercel Dashboard → your project → Settings → Environment Variables
2. Add MAINTENANCE_MODE = true
3. Click Redeploy (no code change needed)

To deactivate: Delete the variable or set it to false → Redeploy.

The maintenance page shows a WhatsApp CTA so delegates can still reach you while the portal is down.

🎫 Badge & Document Management

Generate, preview, email and attach files per delegate

🎫 Generating a Badge

Steps:

2. Find the delegate row
3. Click 🎫 to download badge PDF — saves a file automatically

The badge uses the iOS Blossom theme matching the system — light background, blush pink and sky blue accents.

🎰 Raffle Game Operations — Step-by-Step Playbook

Full guide for running the nomination & raffle game at the convention

📡 Public Game URLs

ENTRY & VOTING

africa-convention.vercel.app/raffle

Delegates scan their badge QR or enter a ticket ID to vote for a fellow delegate

WINNERS REVEAL

africa-convention.vercel.app/raffle/reveal

Display screen for the venue projector — automatically shows animated reveal once winners are set

1Open Voting

Before the session begins, go to the Raffle tab in the admin panel.

Tap 🗳️ Open Voting. The button turns green and the status badge changes to Open.

Delegates can now visit /raffle, scan their badge QR or enter their Ticket ID, and cast a vote for a fellow delegate.

2Display QR Codes at Venue

The Raffle tab shows two large QR code images ready to print or display on screen:

Entry QR — links to /raffle (voting page)
Reveal QR — links to /raffle/reveal (results page)

Print these or project them so delegates can scan without typing the URL. Place entry QR at registration desks and reveal QR on the stage display.

3Monitor Top-3 Panel

While voting is open, the Current Top 3 panel (gold / silver / bronze cards) updates live in the Raffle tab whenever you reload it.

Use this to watch for surges or ties before closing. The leaderboard below shows all nominees with vote counts in descending order.

Nominee cards on the public vote page are numbered 1–N so delegates can easily reference who they're voting for.

4Close Voting

When the voting window ends, tap 🔒 Close Voting in the Raffle tab.

The /raffle entry page will show a Voting Closed status badge and prevent new votes.

The leaderboard remains visible to you — final rankings are locked in at this point.

5Reveal Winners 🏆

Tap 🏆 Reveal Winners. This locks the top-3 result and sets the game state to reveal_ready.

What happens next:
• The /raffle/reveal page fires confetti, emoji floaters, a gold flash, and staggered podium animations
• The /raffle entry page auto-redirects to the reveal page after 2 seconds
• The home page raffle section colours up from greyscale to gold/silver/bronze

Ideal moment: project the reveal URL on the main screen, then tap Reveal.

6Reset Raffle (New Round)

After the ceremony, tap 🗑️ Reset Raffle to clear all votes, nominees, and winners — ready for a fresh round.

Warning: This action is permanent. All vote data is wiped. Only reset when the awards have been presented and you're sure no further tallying is needed.

After reset the game status returns to Closed and all public pages show the default greyscale podium placeholder.
